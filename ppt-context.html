FIELD 4

PS Category — Software / Hardware

Recommendation

Hardware — with a software decision engine as the interpretation layer on top of it.

Why Hardware is the right answer

The core novelty of the project is physical sensing, not the software alone:

  • A perforated probe-lance array sampling interstitial air and vibration at three depths — this is the thing nothing else on the market does for bagged stacks specifically.
  • A single shared CO₂ analyser multiplexed across ports via a rotary manifold — a genuine hardware engineering decision, not a software one.
  • Contact piezoelectric and MEMS microphone sensing for insect detection.

The software — the digital twin, the decision engine, the comparison mode — exists to prove the interpretation logic works. It is real code, but it is in service of a hardware product. If your portal only allows one category, Hardware is the more accurate and more defensible choice; picking Software would undersell the actual proposal.

Say this if a judge asks why you demoed mostly software

“The sensing hardware is largely commodity components — the hard engineering problem is the interpretation logic that turns noisy readings into a trustworthy prediction. That is what we built and validated first. Hardware fabrication and lab-scale validation are the immediately following phase, and they are scoped and budgeted in our proposal.”

This is a recommendation based on the project’s own proposal, not a rule. Confirm against whatever categories your specific portal actually offers.

FIELD 6

Methodology & Process for Implementation

1 · The core control loop

The system is architected as a closed loop, not a one-way dashboard. This is the diagram to put on your methodology slide:

Sense
Interstitial CO₂, humidity, temperature and acoustic emission, sampled at multiple depths
→
Interpret
Convert raw readings into water activity, growth kinetics and anomaly scores
→
Decide
Composite risk score, ranked zones, an action card with a deadline
continuous loop↻
Verify
Post-intervention signature decay confirms the treatment worked
←
Act
Aeration, turning, or targeted fumigation of the identified stack

This closes a loop that current practice leaves open at both ends — nothing today verifies that a treatment actually worked, so failures are typically only discovered when infestation returns.

2 · Interpretation pipeline — physics to biology

The step that converts a raw sensor number into an actionable prediction, in the order it actually executes:

  1. Water activity — measured interstitial relative humidity at equilibrium approximates grain water activity, via the sorption isotherm.
  2. Fungal growth kinetics — water activity and temperature feed a cardinal-value growth model, giving days-to-onset for each zone.
  3. Respiration anomaly — CO₂ is compared differentially against the other ports at the same depth on the same instrument, which cancels drift and slow ventilation changes.
  4. Insect classification — acoustic streams are filtered for impulse rate and spectral signature, then matched against published species profiles.

3 · Working prototype

This is not a mockup. It is a running, self-checking simulation with real finite-difference physics, a real sorption isotherm, and a real decision engine, verified against headless assertions before every session.

4 · Physical validation — what is real, not simulated

To avoid the project resting on simulation alone, a physical water-activity measurement was run in parallel using the sensing hardware actually built:

  • Two sealed jars of maize, identical except for moisture content — one at storage moisture, one deliberately wetted.
  • An SHT3x sensor, on the same ESP32 firmware built for the field probe, reads the headspace of each jar.
  • The measured relative humidity is fed into the same decision-engine code used by the simulation — one source of truth for the science, not two.
  • Result: the dry jar returns water activity below the 0.82 growth floor; the wet jar returns water activity above it, with a predicted mould-onset timeline.
State this boundary explicitly on the slide

The jars demonstrate a measured water-activity difference through real hardware and real code. They do not yet demonstrate observed spoilage or a measured CO₂ signal — those require a longer incubation window and a CO₂ sensor not yet sourced. Being precise about this distinction is stronger than blurring it.
