The problem

Spoilage starts where

1 · Localised moisture

Roof leaks, wet bags, condensation or a damp corner raise moisture in one small region inside a stack — not across the whole of it.

02
The insight

Spoilage breathes.
Gas arrives first.

One reaction, three products

Grain is alive, and so are the fungi on it. Both consume sugar and oxygen and release CO₂, water and heat — from a single reaction.

But those three products travel very differently. CO₂ escapes through the air gaps between kernels. Heat must conduct through the kernels themselves, and grain is an excellent insulator.

A compost heap, buried

You smell a compost heap across the garden long before you could feel its warmth by touching it. A spoiling pocket is the same event — smaller, slower, and out of sight.

Time to travel the same 0.4 m
CO₂ 0.9 days
Heat 15.4 days
Moisture 123.5 days

Computed as t = L²/D from published transport coefficients. Because both times scale with distance squared, the ratio holds at any separation.

The core idea

Not all water in a kernel is usable — some is chemically bound to starch and protein, and fungi cannot reach it. Water activity is the free fraction, 0 to 1. At equilibrium it is the humidity of the air between the kernels — which is why an ordinary humidity sensor measures the governing variable.

0.82 — the growth floor

The conservative end of the published range for A. flavus. Reported minima vary with strain and substrate; we deliberately took the lowest credible value, because a warning system should alert early rather than late.

0.85 — the toxin line

Growth begins at 0.82, but aflatoxin production needs conditions wetter still. Two thresholds, not one.

06
The boundary

You can stop the fungus.
You cannot unmake the toxin.

Aflatoxin is a liver carcinogen. Drying does not destroy it. Cooking does not destroy it — it is heat stable. Once aflatoxin is in a consignment, that grain’s fate is decided.
Diagnosis is too late to act on

A system that tells you grain is already contaminated has told you nothing you can do anything about.

So the problem is predictive

The only intervention that changes the outcome happens before the fungus grows. We forecast the conditions rather than confirm the damage.

And we state the limit

We do not measure toxin. We predict the conditions that create it, and we say so in every output the system produces. Confirming toxin needs a laboratory assay.

A system claiming to read toxin off a humidity sensor would be indefensible. Stating the limit is what makes the rest believable.
07
Current practice

The problem isn’t diligence.
It’s reach.

1
Only reaches the outside

A spear reaches roughly 0.4 m. A godown stack is metres deep. The middle is not reachable.

2
Problems cluster

Insects and moisture gather in pockets. A clean sample here says nothing about a pocket two metres away.

3
Early stages are invisible

Eggs and larvae develop inside kernels. A sample with no adults can still be heavily infested.

Why the chemistry is failing too

Indian Tribolium castaneum shows 2.11 to 70.94-fold phosphine resistance (Sci. Reports 13:16497, 2023). Blanket dosing selects for exactly this — and fumigation kills insects; it does not un-rot grain, and it cannot remove aflatoxin.

“A spear reaches forty centimetres. Our hotspot is at one and a half metres from the nearest reachable face. No amount of diligence gets a spear to that location.”

This is not a criticism of inspection. It is precisely the reason an interior probe needs to exist.

09
Architecture

Four layers. One answer:
where, how bad, how long.

Sense
9 ports · 3 lances × 3 depths

One shared NDIR analyser across all nine sampling points. SHT31 humidity, because relative humidity at equilibrium is water activity.

Interpret
Isotherm → growth kinetics

Humidity becomes water activity; water activity and temperature feed a cardinal-value growth model giving days-to-onset per zone.

Network
One node per stack · offline-first

Zero external dependencies. Runs entirely with WiFi off, because rural godown connectivity cannot be assumed.

See
Ranked ports · risk map

Every hotspot with a coordinate, a water activity, a deadline and an accumulated dry-matter loss.

The gotcha we designed around

Every NDIR sensor auto-rezeros to the lowest value it sees. Inside grain it never meets fresh air — so ABC must be disabled in firmware, or the sensor quietly erases the very signal we exist to detect.

Capacity is outpacing the ability to monitor it

Under the World’s Largest Grain Storage Plan, 500+ PACS godowns are under construction. None are instrumented today.

10
Detection

We chose the
explainable detector.

✗  Not a fixed threshold

Godown temperature swings seasonally. Any absolute limit either cries wolf in summer or sleeps through winter.

✗  Not all nine ports together

Depth stratification is real and about 1,000 ppm — deep grain is furthest from a venting face. Comparing all nine buried the signal completely.

✓  Depth-matched differential

Each port is compared only against the other lances at its own depth, on the same analyser. This cancels sensor drift and slow ventilation changes at the same time — and it is the reason a real pocket stands out at all.

The measured separation

Sustained over three consecutive readings — one noisy sample is never an alarm.

The detector runs on deliberately imperfect data: CO₂ ±25 ppm + 1.5 %, RH ±1.0 %, temperature ±0.15 °C — real datasheet noise on every reading.

Schematic — radial dimension exaggerated for legibility. Mid-depth carries the CO₂ port because it avoids edge dilution and gives the most representative interstitial reading.

Shared analyser

Ports feed a manifold into a single NDIR instrument. The expensive part serves nine sampling points — and every depth comparison is made by the same instrument against the same baseline, so drift cancels.

Non-destructive

Perforated lance driven between bag courses. Removable and reusable across stacks — which matters because stacks are built and dismantled.

All 3.3 V I²C by design

13
Intervention

Detect early enough and
you never need the chemical.

Tier 1 · automatic
Aerate

Drive existing godown aeration over the flagged zone. Reversible, non-toxic, cheap. The system watches CO₂ and water activity fall and confirms it worked.

Tier 2 · human hours
Turn / isolate

Pull the affected bags, dry and separate them, return the sound grain. Only possible because you know which bags.

Tier 3 · licensed operator
Targeted fumigation

Fumigate that stack, not the shed. Less chemical, less exposure, less selection pressure for resistance.

Then · and nobody else does
Verify

If the CO₂ anomaly does not fall, the system escalates rather than assuming success. Current practice never checks at all.

Late detection leaves only Tier 3

Applied to everything, on a chemical that resistance is already eroding.

Early detection resolves most events at Tier 1

The fumigant becomes the exception rather than the routine.
