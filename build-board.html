Science changes needing sign-off

five constants moved during calibration
WhatWasNowWhy it had to moveOwns it
Bulk grain moisture 13.5 % 12.0 % At 13.5 % the sound grain respires hard enough to drown the pocket. The system is linear in respiration, so scaling R0 cannot fix it — only drier bulk can. 12 % is FCI wheat acceptance moisture; aw 0.66, far below the floor. Krittika
Aflatoxin temperature ceiling 30 °C 35 °C 25–30 °C is the reported optimum, not the permissive limit. With 30 as the ceiling, a 30 °C godown that self-heated fell straight out of the band and the toxin window never opened at all. Krittika
CO₂ differential basis all 9 ports same depth Depth stratification is real and stable — mid-depth ports sit ~1,000 ppm above ones near a vented face. An all-ports median buried the signal completely. Comparing lances at equal depth is the honest differential. Vishal
Alert threshold 4.0 σ 3.0 σ Measured separation: clean grain peaks at 1.5 σ over ten days, a hotspot reaches 4.2 σ. 3.0 sits with roughly 2× margin either side. Vishal
Conventional detection route spear only + musty stack A spear reaches 0.4 m; the deep pocket sits 1.6 m in and is simply unreachable, so the arm read “never detected” — true to FAO, but it looks rigged. Added a second route at mould index 2.0, giving day 42. Netra

Every one of these is defensible as it stands, but the person who owns the number should be the one who answers for it on stage — not the person who typed it.
